# The BarelySmash Manifesto

> Version 1.0

---

# Technology changes.

Principles endure.

BarelySmash exists to build intelligence systems that improve human decision-making.

Everything else—models, languages, frameworks, and products—is temporary.

Our principles are not.

---

# Our Beliefs

## Humans remain accountable.

Technology assists.

Humans decide.

---

## Intelligence is measured by decisions.

Data is not intelligence.

Metrics are not intelligence.

Predictions are not intelligence.

Intelligence improves decisions.

---

## Every recommendation must be explainable.

Recommendations should always be supported by evidence.

People deserve to understand not only *what* is recommended, but *why*.

---

## Learning never stops.

Every action produces outcomes.

Every outcome creates learning.

Every lesson strengthens future decisions.

---

## Specialization creates expertise.

General systems coordinate.

Specialized systems reason.

Atlas understands operations.

Friday understands markets.

Future systems will understand their own domains.

---

## Simplicity is a feature.

Complexity belongs inside the platform.

Clarity belongs at the interface.

---

## Architecture reflects responsibility.

Every component has one clear purpose.

JARVIS coordinates.

Engines reason.

Applications present.

Infrastructure enables.

---

---

# Our Standard

When faced with multiple solutions, choose the one that is:

- More understandable.
- More explainable.
- More maintainable.
- More composable.
- More useful to the human decision maker.

---

# Our Mission

We are not building software.

We are building a Decision Intelligence Platform.

Everything else is implementation.

---

## BarelySmash Motto

**Observe.**

**Understand.**

**Decide.**

**Learn.**

**Repeat.**
