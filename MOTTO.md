## Our Measure of Success

Success is not measured by the sophistication of our software.

It is measured by whether people consistently make better decisions because our systems exist.

# BarelySmash Motto

Observe.

Understand.

Decide.

Learn.

Repeat.

---

Observation creates understanding.

Understanding enables decisions.

Decisions create outcomes.

Outcomes become learning.

Learning improves future decisions.

---

Technology changes.

Principles endure.
