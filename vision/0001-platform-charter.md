# BarelySmash Platform Charter

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

BarelySmash exists to help people make better decisions through specialized intelligence systems.

The platform is not built around a single product.

It is built around a clear separation of responsibilities:

- Engines produce decisions.
- Applications present decisions.
- JARVIS coordinates decisions.

---

# Why JARVIS Exists

JARVIS is the executive orchestrator.

It coordinates specialized intelligence engines and presents a unified briefing to the user.

JARVIS does not own trading logic.

JARVIS does not own operational logic.

JARVIS routes, prioritizes, schedules, remembers context, and synthesizes.

---

# Why Atlas Exists

Atlas exists to understand operations.

It transforms operational data into decisions.

Atlas powers applications such as:

- RestaurantOS
- WineryOS
- HotelOS
- RetailOS

---

# Why Friday Exists

Friday exists to understand markets.

It transforms market data into trading decisions.

Friday powers applications such as:

- BarelyTrade
- future trading products

---

# Why Applications Exist

Applications make engine decisions useful.

They present intelligence to specific users in specific workflows.

Applications do not create intelligence directly.

---

# Why Foundation Exists

Foundation provides shared technical infrastructure.

Examples:

- configuration
- logging
- scheduling
- notifications
- authentication
- storage
- LLM adapters
- telemetry

Foundation does not own business logic.

---

# Platform Principle

Every component should have one clear responsibility.

If a responsibility crosses domains, it belongs in Foundation.

If it creates operational decisions, it belongs in Atlas.

If it creates trading decisions, it belongs in Friday.

If it coordinates decisions, it belongs in JARVIS.

If it presents decisions, it belongs in an application.
