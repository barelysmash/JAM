Supersedes: none

Superseded by: none

## Context

What situation forced a choice.

Write this so it stands on its own. A reader two years from now has not been in
the conversation that produced it, and the pressures that made this decision
hard may no longer be visible in the system.

State the constraints that were real at the time, including the ones that have
nothing to do with engineering — deadlines, headcount, an existing commitment
that could not be broken.

---

## Decision

What was decided. One or two sentences, in the present tense, stated as a rule
the system follows.

Not "we will consider" or "we should". A decision that hedges is still a
discussion.

---

## Rationale

Why this option and not the others.

Numbered points. Each one should be a reason that would independently trouble
someone arguing for an alternative.

---

## Alternatives Considered

Each alternative that had a serious case, and the specific reason it lost.

An alternative dismissed in half a sentence was not a serious alternative and
does not need to appear here. If every alternative was dismissed that easily,
this is probably not an ADR.

---

## Consequences

**Positive.** What this buys.

**Negative.** What this costs. Be concrete. This section is the one a future
reader will check first, because they are reading the ADR precisely when the
cost has started to bite.

**Neutral.** What changes without being clearly better or worse, including work
this decision defers to someone else.

---

## Open Questions

What this decision deliberately leaves unresolved, and what would need to be
true to resolve it.

An empty section here is suspicious. Most decisions of this size leave
something dangling.
